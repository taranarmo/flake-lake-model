
Temperature profiles in lake bottom sediments.
Data from measurements in Lake Mendota.
Data are taken from the Tables given in the pioneering paper 
Birge, E. A., C. Juday, and H. W. March, 1927:
The temperature of the bottom deposits of Lake Mendota,
Trans. Wiscon. Acad. Sci., vol. 23, pp. 187-231.

Data are presented in two columns,
first column    -  depth below the water-sediment interface, m,
second column   -  temperature, degrees C.
