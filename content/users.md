## FLake Users Worldwide

FLake is utilized by national meteorological services, environmental agencies, limnological research centers, and academic universities across Europe, North America, and globally.

- **School of Geography, Planning & Environmental Policy, University College Dublin**

(UCD-GPEP), Dublin, Ireland FLake is used in developing a theoretical model on subaqueous weathering in lakes in periglacial environments.
