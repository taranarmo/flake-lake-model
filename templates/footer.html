About FLake

FLake (Freshwater Lake model) is a computationally efficient bulk model capable of predicting the vertical temperature structure and mixing conditions in lakes of various depths on time scales from hours to multi-year climate runs.

Developed through collaboration between the German Weather Service (DWD) and the Leibniz Institute of Freshwater Ecology and Inland Fisheries (IGB Berlin), FLake is used operationally in numerical weather prediction (ICON, COSMO, ECMWF IFS) and climate modeling worldwide.
